# RESULTS-2026-09-13-c3: AFTR TCP mapping idle lifetime

Status: measured. The AFTR expires an idle TCP mapping in the window
(120, 300] seconds on this node and session: alive through 120 s of
silence, dead by 300 s. This is the measurement the plan/0004
UDP-only constraint names as the unlock for TCP grants, and it sizes the
TCP datapath's keepalive model.

Redaction note: addresses are placeholders; the raw data stays on the
router at /mnt/nvme/runs/2026-09-13-c3e.

## Method

Per gap, the sink holds an AFTR TCP mapping (STUN-over-TCP against the
external STUN server, egress pinned to the eth1 line), leaving the
mapping silent for the gap. The probe then connects to the mapped tuple
from the vdsl4 vantage. Alive is scored from the eth1 SYN arrival
capture (the soak campaign's arrival methodology): the AFTR forwarding
the probe's SYN to the inner tuple means the mapping is alive, exit 0 on
the first forwarded SYN. The probe's own connect cannot signal on the
box: the fw4 input chain drops forwarded TCP NEW silently, so the
connect times out even while the mapping is alive, and only shows
refused once the AFTR itself RSTs a dead mapping.

## The death point

The mapping survives 120 s of silence and is gone by 300 s: the AFTR's
TCP mapping idle lifetime on this node and session lies in (120, 300]
seconds. The 300 and 600 s readings are double-confirmed: no forwarded
SYN on eth1 (capture absence) and the probe sees refused (the AFTR RSTs
an unmapped port, the C8 signal). A narrower point would need a
binary-search pass between 120 and 300 s; the bound is the substance.

## Scoping

One node, one session, one load. The AFTR's state is node and load
dependent (the UDP side showed the same variance: 5 to 10 s TTL on other
nodes versus >30 s silent survival today). The bound holds for this
session only.

## Contrast with UDP

UDP silent survival exceeded 30 s (the probe-quiet run-3 measurement)
and the AFTR refreshes UDP mappings on inbound datagrams (runs 1 and 2).
TCP died between 120 and 300 s of silence here, with no inbound-refresh
observed. The channel idle semantics differ materially: the TCP
datapath must treat a TCP mapping as expected to expire after minutes of
silence and re-establish on demand, rather than keep-alive it like UDP.

## Unlock

plan/0004's constraint "no TCP grants until the TCP idle-lifetime test
is measured" is discharged: the test has run and the lifetime is bounded
above by 300 s. TCP grants enter the milestone's datapath task
(#tcp-datapath), whose kept-desired model is sized by this bound.
